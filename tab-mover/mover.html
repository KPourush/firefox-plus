Tab Mover

1. Export (old Firefox)

Saves every open tab, in tab order, with its title and link.

2. Import (Firefox+)

Pick the exported file, or paste an export or a shared list of links below.

Mark group members (optional)

Paste the links you shared from the big group. Matching tabs get #ffg added to their link, so searching #ffg in Firefox+ finds exactly those tabs for Select all → Add to group.
